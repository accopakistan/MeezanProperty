## Rules followed

- **Source access.** Zameen's `robots.txt` was checked, and only allowed paths were read. Graana's `robots.txt` returned 403, so nothing was read from it. SBCA's `robots.txt` returns a server error, so its pages were read once by hand and no automated collection is on.
- **Claims.** A developer's or portal's claim is a labelled fact, not verification. Nothing rests on a search snippet.
- **Categories.** SBCA and Zameen don't state a use in the Index's terms, so categories from them are marked inferred. "Tower" in a name doesn't make a business tower: from this round, only Citadel 7, Chohan Tower, Studio Corporate Offices and two names from SBCA with office wording (Country Finance Tower, Indigo Business Centre) were placed there.
- **Duplicates.** Names were matched to existing records. Where one building appears twice on SBCA's list under an old and a new name, the later NOC was kept. 33 NOCs for blocks of KN Gohar Green City are one entry.
- **Accuracy.** SBCA rows came through a text conversion of the page, so staff should compare them with SBCA's page when checking them. All of them wait for that check.
