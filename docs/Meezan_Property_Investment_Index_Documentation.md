## 1. What it is

The Investment Index ranks **real estate developments only**, in five categories:
- Housing Societies;
- Commercial Plazas;
- Apartment Buildings;
- Shopping Malls;
- Business Towers (office buildings included).

Individual plots, houses, shops, apartments and listings stay in the marketplace and are never ranked.

**How scores are made:**
- **Verified evidence only:** every score comes from verified, traceable evidence, meaning official records, inspected or audited documents, and owner documents checked by staff.
- **Missing stays missing:** a factor without evidence is never estimated or generated by AI.
- **Not enough evidence:** a project without enough of it shows **Insufficient Verified Data**.
- **Legal gate:** a serious legal or safety finding excludes a project from recommendations.
- **Independent:** nothing is paid or sponsored.
- **Automatic:** scores are recalculated automatically after every verified change.

## 2. How it works

```
   │ authorized once, read daily 03:45, polite                │ submitted on the project page
   ▼                                                           ▼
registry entries ──match (same city, same phase)──► project ◄── staff check once (Admin → Owner documents)
   │                                                           │
   ▼                                                           ▼
verified legal records (official source, row URL, time)   verified evidence (source, URL, time, document hash)
   └─────────────────────────────┬─────────────────────────────┘
                                 ▼  any verified change → queued recalculation (and daily 04:30)

               published run → leaderboards · Overall Index · profiles · change history
```

## 3. Scoring

**Weights:** per category, as specified in the brief, editable only through a new methodology version (§6).

**How each factor is scored:**
- **Score range:** each factor is scored 0–100 only from verified records of accepted source types.
- **Price history:** from verified transaction or valuation prices only. Asking prices never count.
- **Rental yield:** a verified gross yield, or a verified lease rent paired with a verified price or valuation.
- **Developer track record:** the delivery record of the developer's other projects.
- **Age limit:** evidence older than 36 months isn't used.

**Project score:** the weighted average of the verified factors, reduced by up to 20% for weight without evidence.

**Project statuses:**

| Status | When |
|---|---|
| **Excluded** | An official cancellation, illegal-scheme listing, restriction or serious finding not cleared later by the same authority, or a verified unsafe building |
| **Insufficient Verified Data** | Legal status not verified, under 60% of the weight evidenced, or Data Confidence Score below 35 |
| **Not eligible** | Approval only pending: scored, not ranked |
| **Ranked** | Everything above satisfied |

**Data Confidence Score:** coverage 30%, recency 25%, source authority 25%, independent publishers 10%, corroboration 10%. It's shown separately from the score.

**Risk classes:** low, moderate, elevated, high, built from verified findings (pending approval, disputes, delays, closure, low occupancy, weak finances, safety deficiencies, a weak developer record, thin evidence), or excluded.

**Overall Index:** within each category that has at least 3 ranked projects, a project's mid-rank percentile is its normalized score. The Index is that score × (0.5 + 0.5 × confidence ÷ 100). Unlike assets are never compared by raw figures.

## 5. Running it (operations)

| Task | How |
|---|---|
| Recalculate now | `php artisan meezan:investment-index run`. Normally automatic: queued after every verified change, and daily at 04:30 |
| Status | `php artisan meezan:investment-index` |
| Add the built-in official sources | Admin → Investment Index → Official sources → **Add built-in sources**, or `php artisan meezan:investment-collect --setup`. They are added **inactive** |
| Authorize a source (once) | Official sources: access basis, terms page, what you checked, confirm. Read daily at 03:45 from then on |
| Check a source without storing | `php artisan meezan:investment-collect {id} --dry-run` |
| Collect now | Official sources → **Collect now** (queued), or `php artisan meezan:investment-collect {id}` |
| Hostinger | Cron `* * * * * php artisan schedule:run`, with `MEEZAN_QUEUE_VIA_SCHEDULER=true` so queued recalculations and collections run without a worker. Run `php artisan migrate --force` and `php artisan config:cache` after deploying |
| Back out a source | Official sources → **Switch off**. Its records stay as history |

## 6. Admin guide

**Admin → Investment Index:**

| Screen | Use |
|---|---|
| Overview | What's published, verified evidence, registry matches, what's waiting for staff |
| Official sources | Authorize, collect, switch off; collection history |
| Official registry | Every official entry and the project it belongs to; match or reject an entry |
| Exceptions | Unreadable sources or stages, uncertain matches, removals from lists, conflicting official records. Resolve or dismiss with a reason; optionally withdraw a legal record |
| Owner documents | Open each document, check it against the claim, then **Verify**, or **Reject** with a reason the submitter sees |
| Methodology | Draft a new version, edit its weights (each category totals 100; legal approval can't be 0), then **Activate**. The Index recalculates and records the change |

The admin menu badge counts open exceptions plus documents waiting.

## 7. Owner guide (shown on the site)

1. On an in-scope development's page, choose **Submit evidence**. You must be signed in.
2. State one fact and attach up to 3 PDF or image files of 5 MB each:
   - development progress, occupancy, rental income (the yield is calculated from the rent and value you enter), utilities, footfall, tenant quality, maintenance, operations, finances, delivery;
   - or an approval or NOC letter.
3. **Structural safety** needs an independent certificate.
4. Staff check the documents once. Only verified documents count, and rankings can't be bought.

## 8. The 15 mandatory requirements

| # | Requirement | How it is met | Verified by |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Verify legal approvals with official authorities | Legal status only from verified official records; LDA lists collected automatically; owner approval letters accepted only as official records after a staff check | Engine and collection tests; live LDA run |
| 2 | Check development and operational status | Development progress, operational status and delivery status evidence, shown on every profile | Engine, risk and profile tests |
| 3 | Real occupancy and rental information | Verified occupancy and yield evidence from inspections, audits, licensed data or staff-checked owner documents; the yield is calculated from rent and value | Collection and owner-document tests; E2E run |
| 4 | Maintain historical project data | Evidence, legal records, registry entries and every published run are kept; nothing is deleted | Change-history tests; restrict-on-delete keys |
| 5 | Identify legal disputes and regulatory warnings | Court cases, warnings and restrictions as legal records, which deduct from the legal score and add risk | Legal tests |
| 6 | Developer delivery history | Developer track-record factor from the developer's other projects | E2E: "2 projects on record" |
| 7 | Financial and operational risks | Risk classifier: finances, operations, safety, delivery, occupancy | Risk tests |
| 8 | Source URLs and timestamps | Required to verify any record; `collected_at` is DATETIME, so it can't be overwritten | Provenance tests; timestamp test |
| 9 | A separate Data Confidence Score | `DataConfidence`, shown beside every score | Engine and page tests |
| 10 | No AI-generated market data | The engine makes no AI calls; missing stays missing. The AI only words text, grounded in the data | Engine and Gemini grounding tests |
| 11 | Asking prices never as transactions | Price history and yield use transaction or valuation prices only | Engine test |
| 12 | No unsupported rental yields | A yield only from verified rent and verified value | Engine and owner-document tests |
| 13 | No paid or sponsored ranking | The engine never reads featured flags, packages or subscriptions | "Not affected by featured or paid placement" test |
| 14 | Recalculate automatically on verified changes | Queued, de-duplicated job on every verified change, plus a daily run; methodology activation recalculates | Automation tests; E2E run |
| 15 | Complete ranking change history | `investment_index_changes` with reasons, shown on profiles | Change-history and page tests |

**Legal safety rule:**
- **Excluded from positive recommendations:** confirmed serious violations, cancelled approvals and restrictions.
- **Never shown as approved:** an unverified legal status.

**Human review** is limited to exceptional legal, security and source-access matters, plus the one-time document check you chose for owner evidence (4 Nov 2026).

## 9. Known limits

- **Official sources:** only the LDA has collector presets. RDA, CDA and SBCA formats still need checking, and the HTML-table collector can be configured per source.
- **Matching:** automatic matching covers housing schemes. Plazas, malls and towers get legal status through approval letters checked by staff.
- **Map:** no Index map until real projects have positions.
- **Today's data:** no real projects exist yet, so all public results are Insufficient Verified Data.
