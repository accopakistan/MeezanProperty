# Meezan Property: Real-World Project Research Report

Date: 5 November 2026. This builds on the 4 November stage. All sources from both stages are in `MEEZAN_PROPERTY_REAL_WORLD_DATA_SOURCE_REGISTER.md`. Every source here was opened and read. Search results were used only to find sources, except where stated.

## Main findings

1. **Real rankings can't be produced from public websites alone.** Public sources reliably give existence and, for many schemes, legal status. They rarely give occupancy, rent, safety or tenant data.
2. **REIT filings were the best discovery.** They are public, regulated and specific. They gave real occupancy figures for two Karachi buildings, and real construction and sales progress for several apartment developments.
3. **Multan's biggest gap is closed, subject to a staff check.** The MDA list of 61 sanctioned private schemes was read from its scan.
4. **Property portals are only partly usable.** Zameen's `robots.txt` disallows `/property_listings/`, `/search/`, `/indices/`, `/Bahria*` and city paths such as `/Lahore*`, and Graana's `robots.txt` returned 403. No listing page had been collected at this stage, so no asking prices were on file. **Correction, 5 November:** this report originally implied that Zameen was closed to us. Zameen's `/new-projects/` and `/index/` pages are not disallowed, and they were read by hand in the next stage. See `MEEZAN_PROPERTY_REAL_WORLD_RESEARCH_PROGRESS.md`.

## Remaining research gaps, most useful first

1. **Staff check of the hand-read lists**: 362 projects across Multan, Faisalabad, Quetta, Karachi and Peshawar. They count as soon as each list is verified. Multan's 61 is a good place to start.
2. **Karachi**: KDA and more SBCA pages weren't read.
3. **Peshawar**: LUBCA and PDA downloads are blocked by `robots.txt`, so these need permission.
4. **Site inspections or owner documents**: the only realistic route to occupancy, rent and safety evidence, and therefore to rankings.
5. **Licensed market data**: portals' terms and `robots.txt` rule out collection, so asking prices need a licence or a feed.
6. **Coordinates**: none are on file. LDA's data has fields for them, but they are empty on the schemes checked.
