# Meezan Property: Real Property Market Research Report

Date: 6 November 2026.

## The honest position

**No researched portal listing was imported or published, and none will be until there is permission to publish it.**

The brief itself sets the rule: a listing may be published only with an authorised basis, meaning a direct dealer or owner submission, an agency feed, a licence or a partnership. Zameen, Graana and similar portals give none of these:

- **No permission.** Copying their adverts would present a competitor's listing as Meezan's.
- **Database rights.** Zameen's terms (effective 11 January 2022) claim database rights.
- **No access.** Graana's `robots.txt` returns 403, so it wasn't read.

## Why no new listing research was done this round

Reading individual portal adverts across seven cities would only produce private records that can't be shown, while building exactly the copy of Zameen's data its terms object to. **That decision belongs to you.** I didn't take it on my own.

If you want private asking-price intelligence anyway, say so, and I'll collect it in aggregate form (per area and size, like the Zameen Index), not as copied adverts.

## How real listings can appear

1. **Dealers and owners list directly.** Registration, listing, moderation, photos and lead tools all work.
2. **Agency feeds.** An agency that agrees can send a CSV of its own listings. `php artisan meezan:listings-import` loads them into the moderation queue with the source recorded. See the import report.
3. **A licence or partnership** with a portal or data provider, if you choose to pursue one.
