## Why research can't reach a verified rank

The methodology needs verified evidence for 60% of a category's weight. Even a fully verified legal record is only 20–25%. The rest needs:

- development progress
- occupancy
- verified rents
- footfall
- structural safety
- tenant quality
- maintenance

**Public websites don't publish these.** They come from site inspections, owner or manager documents, audited filings or licensed data.

## Weights
