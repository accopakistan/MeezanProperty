## 1. Executive Summary: Why No Project is Ranked Yet

In the published Meezan Investment Index (Run #28), exactly **0 of 1,273 projects** are currently ranked on the public leaderboards.

This is **not** a defect or system failure; it is the correct, mathematically rigorous operation of the Meezan methodology:
1. **Public websites only publish statutory legal status.** Development authorities (LDA, CDA, FDA, SBCA) publish scheme approval lists, layout plans, and illegal scheme notices.
2. Under the approved methodology, **Legal Approval accounts for only 20% to 25% of a category's evaluation weight.**
3. The remaining **75% to 80%** of weight requires operational, on-the-ground facts:
   * Physical construction progress (%)
   * Verified resident / shop occupancy (%)
   * Traceable rental transaction yields (%)
   * Footfall and tenant creditworthiness
   * Utility infrastructure connectivity (Electricity, Gas, Water, Sewerage)
   * Structural building safety certifications
4. **Public regulatory websites do not publish these operational metrics.**
5. Since Meezan Property strictly refuses to simulate, extrapolate, or invent missing data, any project possessing only an official legal approval reaches at most **20% to 25% verified weight coverage**, falling well short of the mandatory **60% threshold**.

## 3. Category Evidence Gap Register

### 3.1 Housing Societies (900 non-demo developments)
* **Current Status:** 515 with verified legal records (LDA, CDA, FDA, MDA, QDA, PDA). Verified weight coverage is exactly **25%**.
* **Gap to 60% Ranking Threshold:** **+35% weight needed**.
* **Primary Missing Evidence:**
  1. *Development Progress (20%):* On-ground earthwork, road paving, boundary wall, and electrification progress.
  2. *Occupancy (15%):* Number of inhabited houses vs total sanctioned plots.
  3. *Utilities (5%):* Grid connection confirmation from DISCOs (LESCO, IESCO, FESCO, MEPCO), SNGPL gas infrastructure, WASA water/sanitation lines.
* **Path to Qualification:** A physical site inspection logging construction progress (20%), resident occupancy (15%), and grid utilities (5%) adds **40% weight**, bringing verified coverage from 25% to **65%**, immediately qualifying the society for an Investment Index ranking.

### 3.2 Commercial Plazas (25 non-demo developments)
* **Current Status:** 11 awaiting staff check. 0% verified coverage.
* **Gap to 60% Ranking Threshold:** **+60% weight needed**.
* **Primary Missing Evidence:**
  1. *Verified Rental Income (20%):* Registered shop leases, actual rental rates per sq.ft.
  2. *Occupancy (15%):* Operating retail and office occupancy percentage.
  3. *Footfall & Commercial Activity (15%):* Customer traffic and business density.
  4. *Tenant Quality (10%):* Ratio of anchor corporate brands vs unvetted tenants.
* **Path to Qualification:** Verified rent roll and manager lease register (35%) + physical site survey (25%) + legal approval (20%) = **80% coverage**.

### 3.3 Apartment Buildings (188 non-demo developments)
* **Current Status:** 147 awaiting staff check. 0% verified coverage.
* **Gap to 60% Ranking Threshold:** **+60% weight needed**.
* **Primary Missing Evidence:**
  1. *Occupancy (15%):* Resident apartment occupancy.
  2. *Rental Yield (15%):* Verified residential lease agreements.
  3. *Structural Safety (15%):* SBCA/LDA vetted structural plans, completion certificates, emergency fire exits.
  4. *Developer Track Record (10%):* Historical delivery track record of the builder.
* **Path to Qualification:** SBCA architectural approval & completion certificate (35%) + resident occupancy roll (15%) + rental yield records (15%) = **65% coverage**.

### 3.4 Shopping Malls (143 non-demo developments)
* **Current Status:** 11 awaiting staff check. 0% verified coverage.
* **Gap to 60% Ranking Threshold:** **+60% weight needed**.
* **Primary Missing Evidence:**
  1. *Operational Performance & Sales Density (15%):* Audited retailer sales per sq.ft.
  2. *Occupancy (15%):* Retail space leased and trading.
  3. *Verified Rental Yield (15%):* Base and turnover rent receipts.
  4. *Footfall (10%):* Turnstile or sensor pedestrian counters.
* **Path to Qualification:** Audited annual REIT or management filings (e.g. Dolmen City REIT filings provide verified occupancy, rent roll, and footfall, immediately yielding 65%+ coverage upon staff verification).

### 3.5 Business Towers (17 non-demo developments)
* **Current Status:** 2 awaiting staff check. 0% verified coverage.
* **Gap to 60% Ranking Threshold:** **+60% weight needed**.
* **Primary Missing Evidence:**
  1. *Office Occupancy (15%):* Occupied leasable corporate floor area.
  2. *Rental Income (15%):* Commercial office lease rates per sq.ft.
  3. *Tenant Quality (15%):* Fortune 500, multinational, or Grade-A domestic corporate tenants.
  4. *Structural & Fire Safety (10%):* Civil defense safety clearances, high-rise structural stability certificates.

## 4. Concrete Roadmap for Bridging Gaps

To bring the first batch of developments onto the public Investment Index leaderboards without violating methodology principles, the following four acquisition tracks are established:

1. **Owner & Manager Document Submissions (`/projects/{project}/evidence`):**
   * Mall managers (e.g. Dolmen, Packages Mall, Centaurus) and corporate tower operators submit audited annual statements and rent rolls.
   * Staff audit documents in the Verification Centre (`admin/investment-index/verification`).
2. **Standardized Site Engineering Surveys:**
   * Meezan inspection officers deploy standard inspection checklists to evaluate 20 Lahore societies (earthwork progress, road asphalt, resident house counts, electric grid transformers).
   * Generates `site_inspection` evidence records (authority 0.75), yielding +40% weight.
3. **Public Utility Verification Integrations:**
   * Verification of electrification status via LESCO / IESCO / FESCO feeder connections.
4. **REIT Financial Statement Harvesting:**
   * Extraction of audited quarterly and annual reports for publicly listed REITs (Dolmen City REIT, Signature Residency REIT, Globe Residency REIT), which already disclose verified occupancy and distributions.
